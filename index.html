Why us
— Plate 004 Research bench, 2024
Five reasons

Why the best formulators keep coming back.

We don't put an ingredient in a formula because it sounds good at a trade show. Every ingredient has a research program behind it — preclinical studies to human trials to published data. When you put our name on your label, it means something.

Two brothers, 1997, the world's first patented Cordyceps sinensis via liquid fermentation. That wasn't a trend — it was conviction. Today, we still translate traditional knowledge into research-backed ingredients.

Clean labels. Consistent quality. GRAS, Novel Food, Kosher, Halal, Non-GMO, Informed Ingredient — the compliance work is done so you can focus on building great products.

Certified B Corp. Plant-based. Ethically sourced. We genuinely believe in what we make — which makes it easier for you to genuinely believe in what you sell. Your customers can tell the difference.

Whether you're launching your first SKU or you're a category leader looking to reformulate, we have the depth of portfolio to support you at every stage. At 25 years in, we're just getting started.
